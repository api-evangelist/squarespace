# Squarespace Pricing

> Complete pricing for all Squarespace subscription plans across 27
> supported currencies. Prices below show what the customer is billed; annual
> plans display the per-month equivalent and the full annual total.

Squarespace offers four plans: Basic, Core, Plus, and Advanced. Each plan
supports monthly or annual billing. Annual billing is discounted relative to
monthly. Prices include any applicable VAT/GST where required by region.

Canonical pricing page: https://www.squarespace.com/pricing

## Squarespace Basic

Entry-level Squarespace website plan. Includes a custom domain (free for one year on annual billing), unlimited bandwidth, SSL security, mobile-optimized templates, SEO tools, and basic analytics. Suitable for personal sites, portfolios, and simple business pages.
