Automated emails send themselves once you've set them up, which makes them one of the most efficient tools in your email marketing strategy. Instead of writing and sending a message every time, you build the email once and let a customer's action, like signing up for your list or completing a purchase, trigger the send. For entrepreneurs and small business owners who are pressed for time, automated emails can help you deepen relationships with your customers online.
