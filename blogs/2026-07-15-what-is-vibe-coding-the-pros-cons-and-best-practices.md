Vibe coding is a way of building software, websites, and UI components by describing what you want in plain language and letting AI generate the code for you. Instead of writing HTML, CSS, or JavaScript line by line, you explain the goal and the AI drafts the technical output. For solo entrepreneurs and small business owners , vibe coding offers a new path to spinning up websites, apps, or features .
