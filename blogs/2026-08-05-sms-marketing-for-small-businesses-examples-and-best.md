Text message marketing, also known as SMS marketing, is a powerful and cost-efficient strategy for small businesses to nurture and strengthen customer relationships . Quick and efficient, text messages reach customers where they are, with 90% of texts being read within three minutes , according to Validity’s State of SMS Marketing report. That means your flash sales, appointment reminders, and other requests have a real chance of getting noticed.
